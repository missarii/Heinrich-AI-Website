Workflow

From request to result

01

Describe the outcome

Tell Heinrich AI what you want built, fixed, or explored. Natural language is enough.

02

Review the plan

In Plan mode, examine the steps, tools, and files the agent intends to touch before anything changes.

03

Approve & execute

Switch to Act mode. Heinrich AI runs tools, edits files, and reports progress while you retain final control.

04

Inspect & continue

Review diffs, checkpoints, and the full action record. Iterate or start the next task with full context retained.

Philosophy

An agent that stays honest

Heinrich AI is designed so you always understand what happened and why. Approvals are explicit. Actions are logged. Checkpoints are recoverable. The goal is not to replace the engineer — it is to amplify disciplined engineering.

Visible control

Sensitive actions require your confirmation. You remain the decision-maker.

Local-first context

Your workspace, secrets, and project state stay under your control.

Recoverable work

Git checkpoints and task history mean nothing is lost if a session is interrupted.
